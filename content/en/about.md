Bijoux Conseil is an independent outlet covering jewellery and piercings.

## Our editorial line

We publish buying guides and comparisons that answer one simple question: how to choose a piece without getting the size, the material or the use wrong. Every article starts from a concrete need, not from a trend.

Three principles guide our content:

1. **Verifiable benchmarks.** The standards we quote (silver fineness, plating thickness, nickel limits) point to official texts, not to commercial habits.
2. **No paid rankings.** Brands are named to illustrate a type of product, never in exchange for anything.
3. **Dated updates.** Every article shows its last modification date.

## What we do not do

We do not give medical advice. Healing, infection and allergy questions belong to a healthcare professional or a qualified piercer. Our piercing articles describe common practice and orders of magnitude, they replace no professional opinion.
