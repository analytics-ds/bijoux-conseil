## Write to the editorial team

For any question about an article, an error report or a topic suggestion, write to **contact@bijoux-conseil.com**.

## Report an error

We correct factual errors and date the correction at the top of the article concerned. Please include:

- the article URL,
- the passage concerned,
- the source that contradicts the information.

## Brands and partners

We do not sell placement in our rankings. Editorial partnership requests go to the same address and get an answer, positive or not.
