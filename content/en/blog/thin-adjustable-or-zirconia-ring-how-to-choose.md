> **In short:**
> 1. Cubic zirconia is a synthetic stone rated around 8 on the Mohs scale, against 10 for diamond: it scratches faster.
> 2. An adjustable ring absorbs one to two sizes, making it the safest format for a gift when the size is unknown.
> 3. A 1 to 1.5 mm band suits solo wear, a 2 mm band and above handles stacking better.
> 4. Above 6 mm of width, a ring feels tighter at the same size: count half a size more.

## The thin ring

A thin band, 1 to 1.5 mm, is the most discreet format and the easiest to stack. It is worn alone or piled on a single finger.

Its limits are mechanical. A thin band warps under lateral pressure: crushed against other rings, caught in a bag handle or in a glove. In sterling silver, softer than 18 carat gold, deformation comes faster.

## The adjustable ring

The band is open at the back, which allows it to be widened or tightened by one to two sizes. It is the obvious answer when giving a gift without knowing the size.

The trade-off is well known:

- **Advantage**: size tolerance, can be moved from one finger to another.
- **Drawback**: the metal fatigues with repeated opening and closing, especially on thin bands.
- **Good practice**: adjust once to the right size, then leave it alone.

## The zirconia-set ring

| Criterion | Cubic zirconia | Diamond |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Synthetic stone | Natural crystallised carbon |
| Hardness (Mohs scale) | Around 8 | 10 |
| Behaviour in use | Scratches and dulls over time | Highly scratch resistant |
| Budget | Accessible | High |

Zirconia gives a sparkle close to diamond to the naked eye, at an incomparable budget. Its weak point is hardness: facets dull over years of daily wear, especially on a ring worn on the dominant hand.

### The setting matters as much as the stone

- **Prong setting**: maximum light, but prongs catch and can lift.
- **Bezel setting**: the stone is encircled in metal, less brilliant but far better protected.
- **Pave setting**: several small stones in a row, sensitive to impact.

## Which format for which use

- **Heavy daily wear, manual work**: 2 mm band and above, bezel set or stone free.
- **Stacking and minimal style**: thin 1 to 1.5 mm bands, several at once.
- **Gift without knowing the size**: adjustable model.
- **Occasion piece**: prong setting, worn occasionally.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between zirconia and diamond?

Cubic zirconia is a synthetic stone rated around 8 on the Mohs scale, diamond is crystallised carbon rated 10. Zirconia scratches faster.

### Does an adjustable ring last?

Yes if adjusted once and then left alone. Repeated opening fatigues the metal.

### Is a thin ring fragile?

It handles solo wear fine but warps when stacked or under lateral pressure.
