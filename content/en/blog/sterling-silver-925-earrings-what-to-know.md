> **In short:**
> 1. Silver 925 means 92.5% pure silver, the rest being an alloy meant to harden the metal, most often copper.
> 2. Tarnishing is a normal, reversible reaction, not a sign of fake silver.
> 3. The hallmark is the only physical proof: without a 925 stamp or a guarantee hallmark, nothing confirms the stated fineness.
> 4. For sensitive skin the point of attention is not silver itself but the 7.5% alloy, nickel in particular.

## What the number 925 actually means

Pure silver, known as fine silver, is 999 parts per thousand. It is too malleable for a piece worn daily: a stud post would bend, a hoop would lose its shape. The industry therefore alloys it with other metals.

**Sterling silver 925** is the standard alloy: 92.5% pure silver, 7.5% other metals. Copper is the most common addition.

### Recognising real sterling silver

Three things can be checked before buying:

- **The 925 stamp** engraved on the post, the clasp or the ring.
- **The guarantee hallmark**, mandatory in France above a certain weight threshold.
- **The stated fineness** on the product page, which commits the seller.

## Tarnishing is not a defect

Silver reacts with sulphur compounds in the air and in perspiration. The grey or black layer that appears is a surface chemical reaction, not wear of the metal.

What speeds it up:

- perfumes and hairspray sprayed onto the piece,
- chlorinated household products,
- open-air storage rather than a closed pouch,
- heavy perspiration.

A polishing cloth impregnated with anti-tarnish product removes the layer in a few passes. Silver dips work but are harsh on stones and plated parts.

## Sensitive skin: look at the alloy, not the fineness

| The 7.5% alloy | Skin tolerance | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Copper | Generally good | Most widespread alloy |
| Nickel | At risk | Capped by REACH |
| Argentium (germanium) | Good | Less prone to tarnishing, more expensive |

A reaction after a few hours of wear, with redness and itching at the contact point, points to contact allergy. A dermatologist settles the question, an article cannot.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does 925 mean on earrings?

Silver 925 contains 92.5% pure silver and 7.5% other metals. Pure silver is too soft to hold a jewellery shape.

### Why do my silver earrings turn black?

Tarnishing from contact with air, perspiration or cosmetics. The layer comes off with a suitable polishing cloth.

### Is sterling silver suitable for sensitive skin?

In most cases yes. The point of attention is the 7.5% alloy, nickel in particular, whose release is capped by REACH.
