> **In short:**
> 1. The relationship sets the format before the budget does: earrings and necklaces for a everyday relationship, rings and bracelets for a close one.
> 2. An engraved piece becomes non-exchangeable, so it is reserved for cases where size and style are known.
> 3. The 30 to 80 euro band is where sterling silver and gold plating become accessible.
> 4. The metal already worn daily is the most reliable style clue, and the one most often ignored.

## Start with the relationship, not the budget

The format of a piece carries a message, and that message has to match the relationship.

| Relationship | Suitable formats | To avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Colleague, acquaintance | Earrings, simple necklace | Rings, engraving |
| Friend | Necklace, earrings, adjustable ring | Unmeasured fixed ring |
| Close family, partner | Any format, personalisation possible | Nothing in particular |

## Formats that avoid sizing mistakes

- **Earrings** have no size. Only weight and material need attention.
- **A necklace** is corrected with a 3 to 5 cm extender chain. A 42 to 45 cm length suits the widest range of people.
- **An adjustable ring**, open at the back, absorbs one to two sizes.

Conversely, the **fixed ring** and the **rigid bangle** do not forgive approximation.

## Personalisation: high value, no margin for error

A piece engraved with an initial, a date or geographic coordinates carries real symbolic value. It also carries a hidden cost: it becomes non-exchangeable, and sometimes unsellable second hand.

Two conditions before committing:

1. know the size with certainty if the format has one,
2. know the metal usually worn.

When in doubt, an unengraved model keeps a way out.

## Budget, read through the material

- **Under 30 euros**: steel, gold coloured metal. Limited durability in daily wear.
- **30 to 80 euros**: sterling silver, gold plated. This is the best durability to price band.
- **Over 100 euros**: vermeil, set synthetic stones.

Two useful benchmarks: **sterling silver 925** contains 92.5% pure silver, and in France the term **gold plated** implies a gold layer of at least 3 microns, unlike gilded or flash plated.

## Observe before buying

Three clues can be noted without asking:

1. the **dominant metal**, silver or gold coloured,
2. the **scale of the pieces** worn, discreet or bold,
3. the **presence of stones**, or their systematic absence.

Giving a metal that is never worn remains the most frequent, and the most expensive, mistake.

## Frequently asked questions

### What jewellery should you give for a birthday?

Earrings or a necklace for an everyday relationship, a ring or a bracelet for a close one if the size is known.

### Should you give personalised jewellery?

Only when size and style are known with certainty, since engraving makes an exchange impossible.

### What budget for a birthday piece of jewellery?

The 30 to 80 euro band gives access to sterling silver and gold plating, with the best durability for the price.
