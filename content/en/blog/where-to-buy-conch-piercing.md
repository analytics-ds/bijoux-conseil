## Why the choice of shop matters for a conch piercing

A **conch piercing** goes through the concha, the central hollow of the ear. It is thick, poorly irrigated cartilage that takes much longer to heal than a lobe. The [ear piercing healing time](/en/blog/ear-piercing-healing-time/) is counted in weeks for a lobe and in months for cartilage.

Throughout this period, the jewellery stays in constant contact with tissue that is still repairing. Buying online without seeing the piece therefore means choosing a shop that clearly states its material, its dimensions and its exchange terms if the size is wrong.

### The three criteria that separate the shops

- **Material**: titanium meeting the ASTM F136 standard, known as grade 23, is the piercers' benchmark for cartilage. It releases no nickel.
- **Dimensions**: a conch ring is most often chosen between 10 and 14 mm in diameter, and the post thickness must match the pierced channel.
- **Remote-buying terms**: being able to exchange a wrong diameter at no cost makes a real difference for jewellery sold by the unit.

The collection splits into two clearly distinct families, which makes the choice easier depending on the state of the cartilage.

### ASTM F136 titanium, nearly half the collection

Eight models are in ASTM F136 grade titanium, available in gold PVD finish or silver-tone titanium, sold from 32 to 40 euros. The brand presents them as hypoallergenic and biocompatible, suited to sensitive piercings as well as healed ones.

Among them, four rings (Amman, Belgrade, Cologne and Varsovie) have a 1.2 mm post and come in 6, 8 and 10 mm, up to 12 mm for Amman and Belgrade. The 10 and 12 mm diameters match the most common sizes for a conch. The other titanium models have a 7 mm long post, 1.2 mm thick.

The material determines when a piece can be worn. This table matches the materials found in the ranking with their use on a conch.

| Solid 14 or 18-carat gold | Obsidian Piercing, Maria Tash | Healed conch | High budget |
| Gold-plated 925 silver | Pohésia, Nébuleuse Bijoux | Healed conch only | Thinner post, piercer's advice recommended |
| 304 stainless steel | Nébuleuse Bijoux (part of the range) | Healed conch, tolerant skin | Less recommended than titanium for cartilage |

Nickel is the leading cause of contact allergy linked to jewellery in Europe. The EU REACH regulation limits nickel release from jewellery inserted into a pierced part of the body to less than 0.2 microgram per cm² per week, a stricter threshold than the 0.5 microgram applied to jewellery simply worn on the skin (REACH Annex XVII, entry 27, [consolidated text published by ECHA](https://www.echa.europa.eu/documents/10162/3bbe9024-52a6-8e63-5581-e686331eb459)).

For jewellery that only touches the lobe, the guidance in the article on [sterling silver 925 earrings](/en/blog/sterling-silver-925-earrings-what-to-know/) still applies. On cartilage, titanium keeps the edge.

### First jewellery change

This is the trickiest moment. The cartilage can still react, and the ring diameter must leave enough room without spinning loosely. ASTM F136 titanium and the option to exchange a diameter at no cost make Pohésia the safest choice at this stage.

### Healed conch

Once the conch is stable, the whole ranking becomes an option. The choice then comes down to style and budget: solid gold at Obsidian Piercing or Maria Tash, low-priced gold finishes at Pohésia or Nébuleuse Bijoux.

## Mistakes to avoid when buying a conch piercing online

1. **Ordering initial piercing jewellery online.** The first piece is supplied and fitted by the piercer, in sterile conditions.
2. **Confusing titanium and steel.** A product page that does not mention the ASTM F136 standard does not guarantee implant-grade titanium.
3. **Ignoring post thickness.** A 0.8 mm post does not replace a 1.2 mm post without the piercer's advice.
4. **Choosing the diameter at random.** Measure or ask the piercer before ordering, a ring that is too tight compresses the cartilage.
5. **Not reading the returns policy.** At some shops, a piece taken out of its sealed pouch is no longer taken back.
