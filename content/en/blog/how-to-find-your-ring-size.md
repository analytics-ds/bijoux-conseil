> **In short:**
> 1. In France, ring size is the inner circumference in millimetres: size 52 means a 52 mm finger circumference.
> 2. Two home methods suffice: a non-elastic string measured with a ruler, or the diameter of an existing ring multiplied by 3.14.
> 3. Measure late in the day at normal temperature, since a finger measured on a winter morning easily gives half a size less.
> 4. When in doubt, the larger size can be corrected, the smaller one will not pass the knuckle.

## What the number means

French ring size is directly the **inner circumference in millimetres**. A size 54 ring has a 54 mm inner circumference. This system is more readable than US sizes, which follow an arbitrary scale.

| France / Europe | Inner diameter | US |
|---|---|---|
| 48 | 15.3 mm | 4.5 |
| 50 | 15.9 mm | 5.25 |
| 52 | 16.6 mm | 6 |
| 54 | 17.2 mm | 6.75 |
| 56 | 17.8 mm | 7.5 |
| 58 | 18.5 mm | 8.25 |

## The two home methods

### The string method

1. Wrap the base of the finger with a non-elastic string or paper strip.
2. Tighten without compressing, the string should still slide.
3. Mark the overlap point with a pen.
4. Unfold and measure the length with a ruler: the result in millimetres is the size.

Watch out: the string must pass the **knuckle**, not just the base of the finger. If the knuckle is wider, it sets the minimum size.

### The reference ring method

Lay a ring that already fits flat, measure its **inner diameter** in millimetres, then multiply by 3.14. A 17 mm diameter gives about 53.4, so a size 53 or 54.

## The most common traps

- **Measuring in the morning.** Fingers are at their thinnest on waking.
- **Measuring in cold weather.** Cold noticeably reduces finger circumference.
- **Forgetting the knuckle.** A thin base with a wide knuckle means the knuckle sets the size.
- **Ignoring band width.** A wide band feels tighter than a thin one at the same size. Count half a size more above 6 mm of width.
- **Transposing between fingers.** Each finger has its own size, and the dominant hand is often slightly larger.

## And adjustable rings

An adjustable ring, open at the back, absorbs one to two sizes of difference. It is the safest option for a gift when the size is unknown. The trade-off: the metal fatigues if it is opened and closed often, especially on thin bands.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you find your ring size?

French size equals finger circumference in millimetres. Either a non-elastic string measured with a ruler, or an existing ring's diameter multiplied by 3.14.

### What time of day should you measure your finger?

Late in the day at normal temperature, when the finger is at its usual volume.

### What if you are between two sizes?

Take the larger size, correctable with a reducer, except for wide band rings.
